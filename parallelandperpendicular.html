What is a Line Segment?

The building block of every shape

Key Idea

  • A line segment is part of a line with two endpoints — it has a clear start and finish.
  • A line goes on forever in both directions.
  • A ray has one endpoint and goes on forever in one direction.
  • We name a segment by its endpoints, like AB or CD.
Why does this matter? When two line segments meet or sit near each other, they form special relationships — parallel or perpendicular. Let's explore!

Parallel Line Segments

Same direction · Always the same distance apart · Never meet

Properties

  • They point in the same direction.
  • The distance between them is always the same.
  • They will never cross or touch, no matter how far you extend them.
  • If two segments are both perpendicular to the same line, they are parallel to each other.
Think about it: Train tracks, the sides of a door, the rungs of a ladder — all parallel!

Perpendicular Line Segments

Cross at exactly 90° · Form a perfect right angle

Properties

  • They cross or meet at exactly 90° (a right angle).
  • Four right angles are formed at the meeting point.
  • The sides of a square meet perpendicularly at every corner.
  • The plus sign + is made of two perpendicular segments.
Think about it: A window cross, the corner of a book, a plus sign — all perpendicular!

Why Triangles Matter

Triangles are everywhere around us! Let's explore why learning about them is so important.

Architecture & Buildings

Roofs, bridges, and towers use triangles because they are the strongest, most stable shape.

Engineering & Bridges

Engineers use triangle frames (trusses) to build strong bridges that don't wobble or collapse.

Navigation & Maps

Surveyors measure triangle angles to find distances and create accurate maps of the land.

Art & Design

Artists and designers use triangles to create balanced, dynamic, and beautiful compositions.

Unit 2 · Problem Solving · 2.1 Mind Stretcher

The Mystery of 7 Angles

Problem: A quadrilateral AE... is made up of four triangles. We know that m∠w + m∠x + m∠y + m∠z = 120°. Find the sum of the seven unknown angles m∠a + m∠b + m∠c + m∠d + m∠e + m∠f + m∠g.

Look at the figure below:

Four triangles meet at point O. Angles w, x, y, z are outer angles. Angles a-g are inside the shape.

Step 1 · Understand

What do we know?

The shape has 4 triangles meeting at point O. Each triangle has angles summing to 180°. The 4 outer angles (w, x, y, z) sum to 120°. We need to find the sum of the 7 unknown angles.

Step 2 · Plan

What's our strategy?

1) Find total angle sum of all 4 triangles. 2) Subtract the known angles (w+x+y+z = 120°). 3) Subtract the angles around point O (360°).

Step 3 · Solve

Let's calculate!

Total of 4 triangles:
4 × 180° = 720°
Subtract outer angles:
720° − 120° = 600°
Subtract angles around point O:
600° − 360° = 240°

The sum of the seven angles is:
240°
Problem 4 · Check the Answer

Checking Our Work

Let's verify the answer 240° using a different method.

The same figure viewed differently: four triangles inside a shape.

Method: Sum of all four triangles

Each triangle has an angle sum of 180°. With 4 triangles: 4 × 180° = 720°
The 720° includes: (1) the 4 outer angles w+x+y+z = 120°, (2) the 4 angles around point O = 360°, (3) the 7 unknown angles we want.
720° − 120° − 360° = 240° ✓
Our answer is correct!

Problem 5 · Another Method

Comparing Two Methods

There's often more than one way to solve a problem! Let's compare.

Vertically opposite angles are equal when two lines cross.

Method What we use Steps Result
Method A 4 triangles + outer + center 720° − 120° − 360° 240°
Method B Vertically opposite angles 2 × 180° = 360°, then subtract 240°
Reflection

Which method do you prefer? Why?

Both methods give the same answer: 240°. Some people prefer Method A because it's straightforward. Others prefer Method B because it uses fewer steps. Mathematicians love having multiple ways to check their work!

Find the Missing Angle

Problem: In a triangle, two angles measure 65° and 45°. What is the measure of the third angle?
Solution
Step 1: Angle sum in a triangle = 180°
Step 2: Sum of two known angles: 65° + 45° = 110°
Step 3: Third angle: 180° − 110° = 70°
70°
2

Isosceles Triangle Mystery

Problem: An isosceles triangle has a vertex (top) angle of 50°. What is the measure of each base angle?
Solution
Step 1: Isosceles triangle has 2 equal base angles.
Step 2: Remaining angle sum: 180° − 50° = 130°
Step 3: Each base angle: 130° ÷ 2 = 65°
65° each
3

Right-Angled Triangle Puzzle

Problem: In a right-angled triangle, one acute angle is 35°. Find the other acute angle.
Solution
Step 1: Right angle = 90°
Step 2: Total of 3 angles = 180°
Step 3: Sum of two acute angles: 180° − 90° = 90°
Step 4: Other acute angle: 90° − 35° = 55°
55°
4

Equilateral Triangle Magic

Problem: An equilateral triangle has all three sides equal. What is the measure of each angle?
Solution
Step 1: All angles in equilateral triangle are equal.
Step 2: Total = 180°
Step 3: Each angle: 180° ÷ 3 = 60°
60° each
5

Angle Ratio Challenge

Problem: The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3. Find the measure of each angle.
Solution
Step 1: Let angles be x, 2x, 3x
Step 2: Sum equation: x + 2x + 3x = 6x
Step 3: 6x = 180°, so x = 30°
Step 4: Angles: 30°, 60°, 90°
30°, 60°, 90°
